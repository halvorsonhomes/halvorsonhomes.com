Halvorson Homes is a family-operated design-builder that has been crafting custom homes in Florida since 1995. Over three decades, we've built our reputation on a simple idea: that the people building your home should care about it as much as you do.

As a design-build firm, we manage the entire process under one roof — from initial design and site planning through permitting, construction, and final walkthrough. That means one point of contact, a tighter timeline, and a finished home that reflects your vision rather than a committee of contractors interpreting it.

Our designs are drawn from the ground up for Florida living: open floor plans, seamless indoor-outdoor connections, covered lanais, and materials selected for the climate. Every home we build is custom, and we work closely with each family to make sure it stays that way.

Being family-operated means we don't hand you off. The same people you meet at the beginning are the ones who hand you your keys.
